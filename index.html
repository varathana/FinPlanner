India Income Sources (Tax Resident)
Salary, interest, rental, dividends, debt-fund gains, and other income are pooled and taxed at India's progressive slab rates. Equity STCG/LTCG are taxed separately at flat special rates.
An Indian tax resident is taxed on worldwide income, so US-source interest/dividends/rent/capital gains below are added to the India computation too — even where the US itself doesn't tax an NRA on them (e.g. capital gains with <183 days present). A Foreign Tax Credit under the India-US DTAA offsets whatever US tax was actually paid.
India Slab & Special Rates
Uses the FY 2025-26 new-regime slabs (0% to ₹4L, 5% to ₹8L, 10% to ₹12L, 15% to ₹16L, 20% to ₹20L, 25% to ₹24L, 30% above), a ₹75,000 standard deduction against salary, and the Section 87A rebate (nil slab-tax when slab-taxed income ≤ ₹12L). STCG at 20%, LTCG at 12.5% after exemption. Surcharge, old regime, and HRA/80C deductions are not modeled.
USA Income Sources (Non-Resident Alien)
SPX (and other broad-based cash-settled index options) are IRC §1256 contracts — gains get a mandatory 60% long-term / 40% short-term blended tax character regardless of the (often same-day) holding period, with open positions marked-to-market at year-end. As a capital-gain-character item, this is only taxed in the US for an NRA who is present ≥183 days; it's always taxed in India at slab rates as worldwide business income from derivatives trading.
NRAs owe 0% on US-source interest that qualifies as exempt "portfolio interest" (most publicly-traded bonds/bank deposits). Dividends and non-ECI rental income are taxed via flat FDAP withholding. US-source capital gains are generally untaxed for an NRA unless present 183+ days in the US during the year. As a foreign (non-equity, non-STT) capital asset, India taxes short-term gains at slab rates and long-term gains at a flat 12.5% with no exemption.
USA NRA Withholding Rates
Default 25% dividend rate reflects the US-India tax treaty rate; statutory FDAP rate without treaty benefit is 30%. Combined tax uses the FX spot rate from the Retirement Plan tab.
